All your dev.ps1 work, one page

Pick the folder that contains your projects (e.g. C:\Users\g\Projects). The dashboard scans it for .dev/ folders and aggregates current tasks, daily logs and completed work — entirely in your browser, nothing leaves your machine.

Folders named archive/archived and projects with "archived": true in .dev/config.json are hidden by default.
